Launch setup

Press Reload launcher before each live start. Starting in view waits for confirmed plane detections before countdown. Starting behind the camera uses a timed launch. After countdown, the controller ramps to Launch throttle and holds it for the Launcher release delay before releasing automatically.

Throttle tracking
These adjustments apply during tracking. Vertical position is measured in the camera image. Vertical Kp, Kd, and turn boost start at zero.


When the plane disappears
A missing, low-confidence, ambiguous, or implausibly jumping detection centers roll. Lost throttle applies once the post-release throttle hold ends. Consecutive detections within the association gate can resume tracking during the loss window. After the detection timeout, idle and center are requested and you must start a new session. An unavailable or stale camera stops immediately. A stationary frame counter also times out.
Loss of this page’s heartbeat, leaving this page, Stop on any remote, or the session time limit ends the session. Centered roll is a command, not measured wings-level flight; idle can cause the aircraft to descend or stall.


Control algorithm
Filtered horizontal position and sideways speed determine roll. During tracking, throttle starts at cruise and can add a vertical correction and a turn boost. Launch power applies through acquisition. The combined tracking throttle follows the throttle ramp and remote limit.
