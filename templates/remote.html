REMOTE SENSITIVITY

Throttle response.

Hold W to raise throttle. Release it to gradually return to idle. S lowers throttle at the fall speed. Applies to touch buttons too. The throttle slider also fades after adjustment.

Speed is in percentage points per second (pp/s). At 10 pp/s, throttle fades from 50% to idle in 5 seconds. Set decay to 0 to hold throttle when released. Adjust while stopped.
