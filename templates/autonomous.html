Launch setup

Starting in view waits for consecutive plane detections before countdown. Starting behind the camera uses a timed launch, then waits for the plane to enter view. Throttle stays idle through the countdown, then ramps to launch throttle. Hold the aircraft until “Launch now” appears after the ramp.

Controller parameters
Save while stopped. Feedback rate is the requested controller update rate in Hz. Percentages are absolute calibrated throttle/roll commands and must fit the limits on the manual remote. The horizontal setpoint uses 0 = left edge, 1 = right edge. No altitude, attitude, depth, or return-to-home control is available.


When the plane disappears
A missing, low-confidence, ambiguous, or implausibly jumping detection centers roll and applies Lost throttle immediately. Consecutive detections within the association gate can resume tracking during the loss window. After the detection timeout, idle and center are requested and you must start a new session. An unavailable or stale camera stops immediately. A stationary frame counter also times out.
Loss of this page’s heartbeat, leaving this page, Stop on any remote, or the session time limit ends the session. Dry run uses the same state transitions. Centered roll is a command, not measured wings-level flight; idle can cause the aircraft to descend or stall.


Control algorithm
Filtered plane position feeds a horizontal proportional-derivative controller: roll = direction × (Kp × deadband(setpoint − x) − Kd × horizontal velocity). Roll is limited and changes at a bounded rate. Throttle ramps to the launch setting, then the cruise setting after acquisition. Throttle does not estimate altitude from the picture.
